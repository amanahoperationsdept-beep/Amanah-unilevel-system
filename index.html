The platform

One foundation for connected operations.

AMANAH is being developed as a responsive system with separate administrator and distributor experiences. The goal is to make everyday workflows clearer while keeping access controls and financial records accountable.

01 / PEOPLE

Distributor onboarding

Structured applications, sponsor-code validation, account review, and role-aware workspaces.

02 / PRODUCTS

Product operations

A foundation for product catalog management, order records, inventory locations, and stock movement.

03 / NETWORK

Network visibility

Planned genealogy, member profiles, monthly qualification, and transparent commission workflows.

Built around responsibility

Trust is part of the system design.

The project emphasizes clear permissions, reliable records, and safeguards around financial activity—not just a polished interface.

  • Server-side identity and role checks
  • Database access controls for member and financial data
  • Audit trails and approval controls for sensitive actions
  • Commission calculations tied to eligible product sales, not recruitment fees
  • Refund and cancellation handling designed to reverse related sales volume and commissions
